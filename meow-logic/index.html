Meow Logic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Meow Logic (package id com.tinyexodus.meowlogic) is a cat logic puzzle game by Tiny Exodus. You can play it fully offline. When your device is online, the game uses the network for four things, all described below: anonymous game statistics, crash reports, optional video ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and the optional Supporter Pack purchase through Google Play.

The short version

Your progress is saved on your device. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. It sends anonymous game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) so we can make the game better and fix bugs; they are not linked to your name or your advertising ID and are not used for advertising. Ads appear only when you tap a button to watch a video for a reward; they are served by Google (AdMob), and Google collects certain device and usage data to serve and measure them. The one purchase, the Supporter Pack, is handled entirely by Google Play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (solved boards, best times, daily streak, fish, cats, café items), your settings and whether you own the Supporter Pack in a local file on your device. This file never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the game.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find out which boards are too hard, where new players get stuck and which features get used, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC. It records:

  • what happens in the game: boards started, solved or restarted (with the room and level or the daily number, board size, play mode, time taken, hints used and mistakes), tutorial and Academy lessons completed, cats met, café items bought, changes of play mode and language, when a rewarded video grants its reward, and when the Supporter Pack is bought;
  • the game's language and app version;
  • a random app-instance ID that the game creates on your device. It is not linked to your name, to an account or to your advertising ID, and it is replaced when you clear the game's data or reinstall it;
  • basic technical information such as device model, Android version and screen size, which store the game was installed from, and your approximate location (country, region or city), which Google derives from your IP address.

These statistics are used only to understand and improve the game. The game turns advertising use off in Google Analytics: it does not collect your advertising ID, and the statistics are not used to personalise or measure ads. We do not sell them or share them with anyone else. Google processes them for us (see Privacy and Security in Firebase); detailed records are kept for two months and then deleted. Statistics recorded while your device is offline are sent the next time it is online.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or runs into an error, it sends a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google LLC. A report contains the technical details of the error (the stack trace and a short log of what the game was doing), the app version, device model, Android version, free memory and storage, screen orientation and a random installation ID. Reports are used only to find and fix bugs and are kept for 90 days.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows no banners and no pop-up ads. A video ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward, such as doubling the fish from a solved board, extra hints, a daily treat or another try after losing your hearts. These ads are served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. When an ad is loaded or shown, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID resettable in your device settings,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your interactions with the game and with ads, and
  • diagnostic information about the ad service.

This information is used to serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance, and (where you have consented) tailor them to your interests. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. You can read how Google handles it in the Google Privacy Policy and in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”. The game sends no other data of yours to advertisers. If you own the Supporter Pack, the game gives you the same rewards without loading any ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Supporter Pack is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment; the game only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made (the product, an order reference and a purchase token) so it can unlock the pack and restore it when you reinstall. We never see your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google Play handles purchases.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules notifications on your device for the time you choose. They are created and shown entirely on your device; nothing about them is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn the reminder off in the game's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, a Google consent form for ads is shown before the game first loads an ad, and a Privacy options entry appears in the game's Settings where you can review or change your choice at any time. Regardless of consent, you can opt out of ad personalisation platform-wide via Google Ads Settings or by resetting or deleting your device's advertising ID in the system settings.

For game statistics and crash reports, you can start over with new random IDs at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings (Settings › Apps › Meow Logic › Storage › Clear data — note that this also erases your saved progress). Uninstalling the game stops all statistics and crash reports.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The only runtime permission the game may ask for is permission to show notifications (Android 13 and newer), and only when you turn on the daily reminder. It never asks for location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone access. Its manifest also includes "normal" permissions that Android grants automatically: network access (Internet and network state) so that ads can load and statistics and crash reports can be sent; the advertising ID permission and Android's Privacy Sandbox ad permissions, used by the ads service; Google Play Billing, for the Supporter Pack; vibration, for the small taps you can turn off in Settings; starting at boot, so a reminder you set survives a restart; the install-referrer permission, which tells Google Analytics which store the game came from; and wake-lock and foreground-service permissions, which Google's services use to finish sending data in the background.

Changes

Future changes will be posted on this page and reflected in the Google Play listing before the corresponding update ships.

Back to Blue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Back to Blue (package id com.tinyexodus.backtoblue) is a cozy tower defense game by Tiny Exodus. You can play it fully offline. When your device is online, the game uses the network for two things, both described below: advertising, and anonymous game statistics that help us improve the game.

The short version

Your game progress is saved on your device. The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases. It sends anonymous game statistics (through Google Analytics for Firebase) so we can see how the game is played and make it better; they are not linked to your name or your advertising ID and are not used for advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google (AdMob), and Google collects certain device and usage data to serve and measure those ads — details below. You can avoid ads entirely by playing offline.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked sectors, best wave counts, settings) in a local file on your device. This file never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the game. How many sectors you have freed is also part of the game statistics described below.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find out which parts of the game are too hard, where new players get stuck and which features get used, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC. It records:

  • what happens in the game: missions started, won, lost or left part-way (with the sector, waves, stars, time played, towers built and powers used), tutorial steps, screens opened, Workshop upgrades, whether the story film was watched or skipped, and when a rewarded ad grants its reward;
  • the game's language, your progress (number of sectors freed), the app version and whether it is a test build;
  • a random app-instance ID that the game creates on your device. It is not linked to your name, to an account or to your advertising ID, and it is replaced when you clear the game's data or reinstall it;
  • basic technical information such as device model, Android version and screen size, which store the game was installed from, and your approximate location (country, region or city), which Google derives from your IP address.

These statistics are used only to understand and improve the game. The game turns advertising use off in Google Analytics: it does not collect your advertising ID, and the statistics are not used to personalise or measure ads. We do not sell them or share them with anyone else. Google processes them for us (see Privacy and Security in Firebase); detailed records are kept for 14 months and then deleted. Statistics recorded while your device is offline are sent the next time it is online.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID resettable in your device settings,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your interactions with the game and with ads, and
  • diagnostic information about the ad service.

This information is used to serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance, and (where you have consented) tailor them to your interests. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. You can read how Google handles it in the Google Privacy Policy and in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”. The game sends no other data of yours to advertisers.

Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, a Google consent form for ads is shown the first time the game loads ads, and a Privacy Options entry appears in the in-game Settings where you can review or change your choice at any time. Regardless of consent, you can opt out of ad personalisation platform-wide via Google Ads Settings or by resetting your device's advertising ID in the system settings.

For game statistics, you can start over with a new random ID at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings (Settings › Apps › Back to Blue › Storage › Clear data — note that this also erases your saved progress). Statistics recorded under the old ID are no longer connected to you and are deleted after 14 months. Uninstalling the game stops all statistics.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The game asks for no runtime permissions: no location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone access. Its manifest includes only "normal" permissions that Android grants automatically: network access (Internet and network state) so that ads can load and statistics can be sent; the advertising ID permission and Android's Privacy Sandbox ad permissions, used by the ads service; the install-referrer permission, which tells Google Analytics which store the game came from; and wake-lock and foreground-service permissions, which Google's services use to finish sending data in the background.

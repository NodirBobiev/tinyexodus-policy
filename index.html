Back to Blue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Back to Blue (package id com.tinyexodus.backtoblue) is an offline tower defense game by Tiny Exodus.

The short version

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connections in the game.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked sectors, best wave counts, sound and haptics settings) in a local file on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because no information is collected at all.

Permissions

The game requests no special Android permissions.

Changes

If the game ever adds features that change this policy (for example advertising in a future update), we will update this page and the Google Play listing before that update ships.

Back to Blue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Back to Blue (package id com.tinyexodus.backtoblue) is a cozy tower defense game by Tiny Exodus. You can play it fully offline; the only feature that uses a network connection is advertising, described below.

The short version

Your game progress stays on your device. The game has no accounts, no analytics, and no in-app purchases. The free version shows ads served by Google (AdMob), and Google collects certain device and usage data to serve and measure those ads — details below. You can avoid ads entirely by playing offline.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked sectors, best wave counts, settings) in a local file on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID resettable in your device settings,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your interactions with the game and with ads, and
  • diagnostic information about the ad service.

This information is used to serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance, and (where you have consented) tailor them to your interests. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. You can read how Google handles it in the Google Privacy Policy and in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”. The game sends no other data of yours — accounts, progress, and purchases do not exist.

Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, a Google consent form is shown the first time the game loads ads, and a Privacy Options entry appears in the in-game Settings where you can review or change your choice at any time. Regardless of consent, you can opt out of ad personalisation platform-wide via Google Ads Settings or by resetting your device's advertising ID in the system settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The game requests network access (Internet and network state) so that ads can load. It requests no other Android permissions — no location, contacts, storage, or camera access.

Changes

This policy was updated on 7 October 2026 to describe the advertising feature added in version 1.2. Future changes will be posted on this page and reflected in the Google Play listing before the corresponding update ships.
